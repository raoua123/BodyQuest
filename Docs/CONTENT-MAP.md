# Content map

Each world has two lessons. Every lesson has: a short explanation, a "Remember" cue,
a fun fact (tap to reveal), a safe "Try it" idea and a one-question quick check worth up to 3 stars.

Lesson titles and text live in `Data/SeedData.cs` (database). The extra material (images, cues, facts,
quick checks) lives in `Services/LessonExtras.cs`; world artwork and colours in `Services/WorldArt.cs`.
